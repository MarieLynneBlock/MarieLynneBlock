how it works

  1. Press play (or the space bar) to hear the loop as it stands.
  2. Click any cell to put a note on that step, or click a note to remove it. Each step holds one note, and your changes play straight away — only for you.
  3. Happy? “Send to the README” opens a pre-filled GitHub issue. Submit it and a workflow writes your notes into the shared loop, the piano roll and the header waveform.

Up to changes per issue. The shared loop can take a few minutes to refresh here after you submit.
